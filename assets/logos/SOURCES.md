# Brand logo sources

All SVGs come from **lobe-icons** (@lobehub/icons-static-svg) — an open
collection of AI brand marks — fetched via jsDelivr CDN on 2026-10-11:

    https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@lobehub/icons-static-svg/icons/<name>.svg
    Upstream: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons (MIT for the set;
    the underlying marks are trademarks of their respective owners)

Files: meta.svg (Meta/Llama), mistral.svg (Mistral), qwen.svg (Qwen/Alibaba),
deepseek.svg (DeepSeek), gemma.svg (Google Gemma), openai.svg (OpenAI
gpt-oss), nvidia.svg (NVIDIA), groq.svg (Groq), openrouter.svg (OpenRouter),
moonshot.svg (Moonshot/Kimi), zhipu.svg (Z.ai/GLM), microsoft.svg (Phi).

Trademarks belong to their owners; used only to identify models/providers —
no endorsement implied. Unknown brands fall back to a monogram, never a
wrong logo.
