# Pitch Script (~spoken, 4–5 min)

> "Quick question — when did a todo list last actually check your work? Every
> productivity app on earth is built on the honor system. Receipts isn't."

[Open app — task list, integrity chip visible]

> "You add a task like normal — 'ship the landing page.' But look: there's no
> 'mark complete'. There's a button that says **Submit proof**."

[Submit a real URL]

> "I give it a link. Now watch — it's not asking an AI to take my word for it.
> It *fetches the page itself*, shows me the source card, and only then does a
> model read the fetched text against my claim. Verified, confidence, sources
> — pass, confetti, my integrity score goes up."

[Second task — submit 'lol did it' as text, or a dead URL]

> "Now the fun part. Weak evidence — watch what happens."

[Point at step list → dead source card → verdict]

> "Dead link: struck through, dimmed, labelled — it never disappears, because
> hiding failures would defeat the point. And then — "

[Roast card drops in]

> "— the auditor has notes. A roast, a NOPE stamp, and the task is *locked*
> behind a sixty-second accountability challenge. Do the challenge, get
> another shot. Skip it, stay locked. That's the loop: evidence or nothing."

[Open the pill]

> "One more thing — this is a multi-model app wearing a friendly costume.
> This pill is the whole model router: four strength stops, provider glyph,
> drag it — springy thumb, haptic at each stop. Under the hood it picks the
> strongest keyed model in the tier, retries across Groq, NVIDIA, OpenRouter
> on failures, drops a tier when it has to, and quietly swaps to a vision
> model when you submit a screenshot. It always shows you who answered."

[Final beat]

> "Everything's local — Hive for state, secure storage for keys, no backend,
> no account, no silent AI. The verifier contract is enforced in code, so even
> a hallucinating model can't pass you on confidence alone. Receipts doesn't
> track what you *meant* to do — it tracks what you proved. Thanks."
